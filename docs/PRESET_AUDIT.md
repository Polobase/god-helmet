# Program audit: every program against its source

The library held 198 programs. Each was read against the sources it cites, in the downloaded library (`library/`) or, for the vendor's pages, on the web. This page records for every program what was wrong, what was done, and the page that decided it. The library now holds **176 programs**; 28 are archived in `presets/archive/` and 6 are new.

What the decisions mean:

- **keep**: parameters and evidence grade stand. The program got its `fidelity` and `dose` labels ([PRESET_FORMAT.md](PRESET_FORMAT.md)) and a text that matches the source.
- **fix**: a parameter was corrected to the source: timing, signal, coils, sites or phases.
- **relabel**: the parameters stand, but name, category or description said something the source does not.
- **demote**: the evidence grade was lowered, with no change to the parameters.
- **archive**: taken out of the library. Nothing loads the file any more.
- **new**: a protocol in the library that no program covered.

## How it was done

- Eleven readers took one family of programs each and read the sources those programs cite. Every number that decided something was read on the page image, because the text layers of the scans confuse µT with mT and drop minus signs. Where only the text layer was read, the reader said so, and such a value decided nothing by itself.
- The vendor's pages (Shakti, Shiva, God Helmet sessions) are not in the library. They were read on the web and are cited by address and heading. They are vendor claims, whatever they say.
- A reader's proposal was applied when a page says it. A proposal that was the reader's own inference was not applied; the larger ones are listed [below](#not-applied).
- Every file was checked after every change: the checker (`npm run check-presets` in `site/`), then the firmware's engine against the JavaScript engine on every program.
- A second group of writers then rewrote each program's summary and notes from the readers' findings, so that the text describes the file as it is.

Nothing in this audit was measured on a helmet. Whether the helmet delivers what a file says is the subject of [HARDWARE_AUDIT.md](HARDWARE_AUDIT.md).

## Decisions taken for the whole library

These were decided once and applied to every program they concern. The table further down does not repeat the reasons.

1. **The intensity was not changed.** The readers proposed 25 changes of `uT`, from 0.04 to 5, in both directions, for programs of the same lab. That spread is the finding: the lab states its fields "at the level of the scalp", "over the activated hemisphere" or without any place, and the one outside measurement of a lab-built helmet gives 3 to 10 µT at intracranial distance (Granqvist 2005 p.3). `uT` is the peak 20 mm in front of the coil, about 24 times less than at the scalp. A program at 1 µT gives about 24 µT at the scalp and 0.18 µT at 40 mm, which lies between the two readings. Matching the scalp reading would also leave 10 steps of the output for a waveform ([HARDWARE_AUDIT.md](HARDWARE_AUDIT.md) B1, B3). Each program now records what its source says in `dose`, with the place and the page.
2. **The lab helmet is four pairs on an ellipse.** Four solenoids per side on an ellipse of 7.5 × 5 cm with the long axis at mid-temporal level (Healey 1996 p.2 [202]), each wired to its counterpart, one left-right pair on at a time for 0.5 s (Richards 1993 p.3 [73]; Persinger 2000 p.3 [661] and Fig. 1). The programs had two fixed coils per side or a cross whose long axis is vertical. They now use FFT7, C5, TP7, T9h and their mirrors (76 × 58 mm on the template head) and step through the four pairs. The site names are this project's reading: no paper names a site.
3. **A pair's field goes through the head.** All coils are mounted the same way round, so two coils of a left-right pair driven with the same sign cancel at the midline. The lab drew the pair as one circuit through the head (Persinger 2000 Fig. 1, p.4 [662]). The right side is therefore driven with the opposite sign. Where a paper gives the two sides' strengths (10 to 1, 5 to 1), the weaker side is attenuated, not switched off (Healey 1996 p.3 [203]; Persinger 2000 p.3 [661]).
4. **"Once every 3 s" is the pause.** Persinger 1999 gives 4000 ms between patterns and 380 presentations in 30 min (p.3 [165]): 380 × (0.69 + 4) s is 29.7 min. Burst programs had the bursts 3 or 4 s apart from start to start. They now pause 3000 or 4000 ms.
5. **The "hippocampal" stand-in is a primed burst.** The vendor's hippocampal signal is not published. The lab describes it as one pulse followed 170 ms later by four, 3.8 times a second (Tsang 2004 p.5 [1017]), and draws it for the Shiva God Helmet (Corradini 2014 Fig. 2, p.103 [92]). The programs played four bursts of four pulses (a theta burst), which no source gives for this signal. 34 programs changed. In `murphy-shakti-60min-bilateral-hippocampus-fast` the old train stays, because its alternation was built round it.
6. **The Shiva burst is 289 points.** 289 points of 3 ms, 867 ms (Collins 2014 p.40 [31]; Saroka 2010 p.5 [470]). The lab helmet's is 230 points, 690 ms.
7. **Evidence and fidelity are separate.** A program that copies only the timing of a method (`timing`) or is this project's own design (`construct`) cannot carry the source's grade, because nobody has tested what the helmet plays. Such programs are `unverified`. This lowered the four theta-burst programs from `established`.
8. **Two corrections run through many descriptions.** Gendle & McGrath 2012 was sham-controlled but not double-blind: "the experimenter knew" (p.7 [223]). Only Granqvist 2005 was. And the burst-firing pattern is not unpublished: its shape is printed (Persinger 2000 Fig. 2, p.5 [663]); only its point values were never given as numbers.

## Placements

No placement was added for a new brain target: the targets for which a source gives coordinates were already in the site list ([PLACEMENT.md](PLACEMENT.md)). One placement is new and one is gone:

- **New: the lab helmet's ellipse** (decision 2), from the dimensions in Healey 1996. 31 programs use it.
- **Gone: "Koren-box pairs"**, two fixed coils per side. Every program that used it described the lab helmet.
- `murphy-shakti-septal-vertical` moved from Cz and CPz to CPz and Pz: the vendor says an inch to two inches behind the top of the head.
- `murphy-shakti-back-and-forth-both` has its second phase over the frontal lobes, as the vendor's page says.
- `remote-viewing` moved from the Layout A ring to the Shiva ring: the vendor's page it cites rules out coils above the nose, above the ears and on the bony ridge at the back, which is where Layout A puts three of them.
- The two Corradini 2014 programs moved to the sites of the Collins programs: the same hardware, and the thesis photo shows the same four positions (p.21 [10]).
- The library now uses 47 placements (54 before).

## Open points

Found and not settled. Each is stated in the notes of the programs it concerns.

- **Field strength.** Decision 1 leaves every lab program about 24 times above the lab's scalp figures and 3 to 50 times below Uppsala's figure at depth. The Shiva God Helmet sessions at 1 µT give about 0.06 µT at 60 mm, 17 times or more below the 1 to 2 µT the thesis reports for the centre of the cap (Collins 2014 p.69 [60]).
- **The vendor's chirp and modulated 40 Hz signals exist as words only.** Three stand-ins are in use: a sweep from 40 to 6 Hz (11 programs, among them `god-helmet-classic` and the 60-minute God Helmet sessions), 40 Hz phase-modulated at 5 Hz (the Collins programs) and a plain 40 Hz sine (three Shiva programs). The vendor's page says the chirp "does not change in tone" and shows a generic picture captioned "the frequency gets lower". Nothing decides between them.
- **Step time of the God Helmet sessions.** 125 ms per coil pair is this project's choice; the vendor says "several times per second".
- **Ramps of six values on eight coils.** The Shiva programs step 10, 12 … 20 ms and start again, so the ramp drifts round the ring. The vendor's lecture lists the numbers and names no unit.
- **Booth's 100 ms row.** The paper gives 100, 80, 60, 40, 20, 1, 1, 1 ms; the format plays 100 to 20 ms and starts again ([PRESET_FORMAT.md](PRESET_FORMAT.md)).
- **The sign of the dwell change.** Each program follows its own paper. The Cook 1999 programs lengthen with "+"; the 2003, 2004, 2008 and 2010 programs shorten. Which the lab's software did cannot be settled from the papers.
- **Sculthorpe's modulated 40 Hz.** The rebuilt cycle is 240 ms, 4.2 per second; the paper says "five complete cycles per sec." (p.3 [1033]) and does not give enough to build it.
- **Breaks between phases.** The papers stopped for a questionnaire between the two halves (Persinger 2000 p.4 [662]; Saroka & Persinger 2013 p.5 [399]; Tiller 1994 p.3 [159]). The programs run through; their notes say where to pause.
- **Still coils where the lab helmet may have stepped its pairs.** The three Sculthorpe programs, the 7 Hz point-duration program and the left-frontal program play all their coils together. The papers do not say what the helmet did.
- **Vendor polarity.** `obe` and `murphy-shakti8-tv-chirpbi-amyl` drive the coils of a pair with one sign, against the vendor's rule that a pair is one positive and one negative coil. The first follows no source at all, the second a video.
- **Pauses with no source.** 2310 ms in the two Limbic Workout programs and 3310 ms in `persinger-shakti-arm-tsang-2004` are the old start-to-start timing. No page gives the interval for these signals, so they were left.
- **`murphy-shakti-60min-bilateral-hippocampus-fast`** keeps the old hippocampal stand-in (decision 5).
- **The halo of Burke 2013** is one winding round the head. Both programs step the field from coil to coil instead, which their notes call this project's construction.
- **Sources that could not be read.** Jin & Phillips 2014 (not in the library), Persinger's 2003 book chapter on the helmet (not in the library), the Laurentian correspondence with Uppsala (archive pages unreachable), the pre-registration of Maij 2019 (the library copy is a sign-in page). Statements that rest on them are marked in the notes or were removed.
- **Ids that no longer fit their program** stay as they are, so that links and logs keep working: `persinger-tinoco-ortiz-2014-replication` is not a Persinger-lab study, `murphy-shakti-psi-rv-untested` is about telepathy, `murphy-shakti-lucid-dream-series-chirp-hipp` is not part of the series.

# Library notes: the ring of eight positions

These are reading notes. They were taken in November 2026 while every program of the library was checked against its sources ([PRESET_AUDIT.md](PRESET_AUDIT.md)), and they are published as they were taken: one row per statement, with the page and, where it is short, the passage. Pages are PDF page numbers with the printed page in brackets, for example `p.3 [661]`. The last column says how a statement was read: `image` on the page image, `text` in the text layer only (scans confuse µT with mT and drop minus signs, so no decision rests on such a value alone), `web` on a web page. Statements about the vendor's products come from the vendor's own pages and are claims. Nothing here was measured on a helmet.

### Dwell schedules, paper by paper

The lab's software took two numbers per configuration: the time on the first position and a change applied at each next position, with the cycle starting again after the eighth. The papers call them "commutator rate" and "change in rate". They disagree on what the sign of the change means.

**Patent US 6,312,376 (filed 28 Aug 1998), p.5 col.4 (image).** No table. "(vi) determine the duration the electromagnetic waveform is to be applied to each channel (i.e. sequence interval); and (vii) determine a sequence acceleration". Worked example: "if the sequence limit is set equal to four, the sequence acceleration is set equal to −2 msecs, the sequence interval is set equal to 10 msecs ... the electromagnetic waveform would firstly be presented on channel 1 for 10 msecs, then on channel 2 for 8 msecs, and then on channel 3 for 6 msecs and then lastly on channel 4 for 4 msecs." Sign: plain. Minus shortens.

**Cook, Koren & Persinger 1999, Table 1, p.2 [62] (image; the text layer prints the signs as "12 120 22").**

Worked examples, p.3 [63] (image): "configurations 2 and 5 had durations of 100 ms at each solenoid and a change in rate of presentation of 20 ms. Consequently the field presentation was 100 ms at the first solenoid, 120 ms at the second solenoid, 140 ms at the third solenoid and 340 ms at the eighth solenoid." And: "The pulse pattern that was 200 ms in duration with a rate of change of −2 ms (sequence 3) was accelerated with the activation of each solenoid such that the duration of the field at each pair of solenoids changed successively from 200 to 186 ms." Sign: plain. Plus lengthens, minus shortens. "340" is a misprint for 240 (100 + 7 × 20): the same page says "the numbers of rotations during 2.5 min for configuration 5 would be 1103" and equates 2.6 s with "about 23 ms per rotation", which fits 110.3 rotations of 1.36 s (100 + 120 + ... + 240 = 1360 ms). ## Places on the head that a source states

No source in this family gives a 10-20 or 10-10 name, an MNI or Talairach coordinate, or a measured distance from a landmark for any solenoid. What they give:

- Ring plane: "at the level of the temporoorbital plane" (Cook 1999 p.1 [61]); "the horizontal plane just above the ears and approximate to the epicanthal-meatal line" (Richards 2002 p.3 [673]); "a line between the midforehead and above the ears" (Swann 2002 p.4 [930]); "horizontal plane above the ears" (Tsang 2004 p.1 [1183]).
- Named regions: solenoid 1 "over the left prefrontal region" (Cook 1999 p.2 [62]) or "left frontal region" (Tsang 2004 p.4 [1186]); solenoid 2 "over the left frontal-temporal interface" (P&L 2010 p.9 [793]); solenoid 8 "over the right prefrontal region" (Tsang 2004 p.4 [1186]); the sequence "left prefrontal ... left posterior frontal, left temporal, left occipital and right occipital ... right temporal and right prefrontal" (PTBK 2008 p.10 [16]).
- Size: ring circumference 55-60 cm (Lehman 2016 p.65 [56]); positions "about 5 cm to 7 cm" apart at the scalp (Tsang 2004 p.8 [1190], text); canister 3.5 × 5 cm (Tsang 2004 p.4 [1186]). The patent instead puts adjacent solenoid assemblies "approximately 0.8 inches" apart with the two of a pair diametrically opposed (p.5 col.3).
- EEG electrodes in these studies (recording sites, not coil sites): F7, F8, T3, T4, P3, P4, O1, O2 (Richards 2002 p.4 [674]; Tsang 2004 p.3 [1185]; Booth 2008 p.3 [428]); F7, F8, T3, T4, O1, O2 bipolar for Swann (Swann 2002, p.5 [931], text).
- Tsang 2009 (boxes, not the ring): "placed over the temporal regions (just above the ears)", field "through the brain at the level of the temporoparietal lobes" (p.3 [367]); no site names.
- Murphy's Shiva: "there are no coils above the nose", "none above the bony ridge at the back of the head", "There are no coils directly above the ears" (https://www.god-helmet.com/wp/shiva/shiva_demo/locations.htm, web); no site names.
- Room and heading, where given: Swann faced magnetic east-southeast in a chamber with a resultant field of about 23,100 nT (Swann 2002 p.3 [929], image); the sibling and stranger studies had both people facing south (PKT 2003 p.4 [880], image).

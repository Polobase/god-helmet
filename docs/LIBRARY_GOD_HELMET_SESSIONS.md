# Library notes: the vendor's God Helmet sessions

These are reading notes. They were taken in November 2026 while every program of the library was checked against its sources ([PRESET_AUDIT.md](PRESET_AUDIT.md)), and they are published as they were taken: one row per statement, with the page and, where it is short, the passage. Pages are PDF page numbers with the printed page in brackets, for example `p.3 [661]`. The last column says how a statement was read: `image` on the page image, `text` in the text layer only (scans confuse µT with mT and drop minus signs, so no decision rests on such a value alone), `web` on a web page. Statements about the vendor's products come from the vendor's own pages and are claims. Nothing here was measured on a helmet.

Other descriptions of the same vendor's hardware in the library: Corradini 2014 (same 4 + 4 cap, "1 µT ... to each of the solenoids", plane not stated); Saroka et al. 2010 (64 solenoids, "4 USB audio devices"); Tsang et al. 2004 (Shakti through a "commercial sound card", 1 and 0.5 µT "near the level of the scalp"); Gendle & McGrath 2012 (8-coil Shakti from one stereo output, 0.1 to 1.0 µT at the centre of the headpiece, 0.1 to 0.2 µT even with the program off).

The field figures do not fit together. A coil that gives 10 mG "measured from one coil" cannot give 1 to 2 µT at the centre of the cap, 7 cm or more away. Gendle & McGrath's meter read 0.1 to 0.2 µT at the centre with the program off, so a broadband meter there largely reads mains hum. The true field of the Shiva coils at the scalp is not published anywhere I read.

Consistency check on the vendor's own figures: 9.6 s of field per minute with 4 s of silence after each signal means a signal of about 0.76 s and a cycle of 4.76 s, which is the "once every 4.76 s" that Tsang et al. 2004 report for the Shakti LTP pattern. The Shiva amygdaloid file (0.867 s) would give 10.7 s per minute, the lab file (0.69 s) 8.8 s. This is my arithmetic, not a source statement.

### Session rules

- Two phases, 20 + 20 min or 30 + 30 min. Vendor: "Each one lasts 20 to 30 minutes"; thesis: "either 20- or 30-minute durations of both primer and target fields". The second phase is where the vendor expects the effects.
- Order: a primer on both sides and a target on one side (amygdala left, hippocampus right) in six sessions; a primer on one side and a target on both sides in two (thesis p.70-71 [61-62]). The vendor's list has the same eight.
- How often: at most once a week, not mixed with Shiva or Shakti sessions in the same weeks, six sessions then 21 days off, each session two or three times before moving on. For 60 min the Shiva page allows weekly or two-weekly and no session longer than 60 min; the Shakti page says at most every three weeks.
- Volume: default 25 %; the chirp-right session plays phase 1 "10% higher" and must not be set above 82 %.
- During a session: silence, eyes covered, relaxed, no meditating, no sleeping; an on-screen button skips to phase 2.
- The thesis ran each person once, 40 min, in a shielded acoustic chamber, eyes closed, with no sham group.

### Where vendor and thesis disagree, or one is silent

1. Field strength. Vendor: no number for the God Helmet; 10 mG at one coil for Shakti. Thesis: 1 to 2 µT at the centre of the cap. These cannot both describe the same coil (see above).
2. Coil geometry. Thesis: 5 cm by 8 cm. Vendor: no dimensions.
3. Rotation direction. Thesis and vendor text agree (right counter-clockwise, left clockwise). The vendor's 2021 brain animation and its alt text say clockwise on the right.
4. Coils on at once. Thesis: always one left-right pair. Vendor: "one or both sides".
5. Step time. Vendor: several rotations per second. Thesis: silent for this layout.
6. Hippocampal signal. Vendor: 3-ms points. Corradini's figure of the Shiva field: 1-ms time axis.
7. Phase length on one vendor page: the 40-min hippocampus-then-amygdala session is listed with "Each phase is 40 minutes" (a slip for 20).
8. Effects. Vendor: presence, elation, calm, imagery. Thesis: "no clear pattern of subjective experiences"; an EEG change in the two bilateral-target sessions; more anger after session 3.
9. No disagreement on: the four signal names, the eight combinations, 20 or 30 min per phase, four USB audio devices and eight coils.

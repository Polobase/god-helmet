# Library notes: Shakti signals and placements

These are reading notes. They were taken in November 2026 while every program of the library was checked against its sources ([PRESET_AUDIT.md](PRESET_AUDIT.md)), and they are published as they were taken: one row per statement, with the page and, where it is short, the passage. Pages are PDF page numbers with the printed page in brackets, for example `p.3 [661]`. The last column says how a statement was read: `image` on the page image, `text` in the text layer only (scans confuse µT with mT and drop minus signs, so no decision rests on such a value alone), `web` on a web page. Statements about the vendor's products come from the vendor's own pages and are claims. Nothing here was measured on a helmet.

`W/` stands for `https://www.god-helmet.com/wp/winshakti/demo/shakti_demo/`. Two pages exist only in the Internet Archive (2007 copies of the same demo on the vendor's old domain) and are marked `archive`. Two short vendor sound clips were measured by the reader (`web, audio`); those numbers are measurements of a recording, not vendor statements.

## Shakti as described

### What all versions share

- Drive: a sound card. "The long cord is plugged into the 'speaker out' jack on your sound card" (W/getting_started_basic.htm); "DO NOT use the 'line out' plug" (W/plugging_in.htm). Signals are 16-bit PCM audio files (W/mono_proposal.htm). The coils are "modified commonly available magnetic coils" (same page); no schematic, turn count, size or resistance is published ("Schematics for the Shakti Hardware are not availble in the DEMO version", W/2_coil_hardware.htm, 4_coil_hardware.htm, 8_coil_hardware.htm).
- Coils always work as pairs of one + ("north") and one - ("south") coil (W/coil_polarities.htm). Which physical pole faces the head for + is not said anywhere.
- Intensity is set as a sound-card volume in percent, different for men and women and per type of card (W/sound_card_types.htm, chart at the foot of the page: PCI card 25 % men, 16 % women; attached to the motherboard or laptop 50 % / 32 %; cheap USB 85 % / 55 %). The percentages are not a calibrated field.
- The demo contains no signal files. No point values of any Shakti signal are published.

### 2-coil Shakti (no longer sold)

- Two coils, one + and one -, on one stereo channel. "When you use only two coils, you are limited to mono signals, and you cannot use the two-phase sessions" (W/2_coil_placement.htm). Photo captions: "Can be either stereo Channel (L or R). Signal must be monaural."
- Placement in the photos: for both sides, one coil on each side of the head (so the + and the - coil sit on opposite hemispheres); for one side, both coils on that side (left temporal: + in front, - behind, on a horizontal band; left parietal: - near the top, + low on the side). In words: "put the coils about an inch above each ear (a bilateral temporal lobe presentation)" (W/obe45_consent.htm). For the cerebellum a one-coil headset is "the most convenient" (W/cerebellum.htm).

### 4-coil Shakti (no longer sold)

- Four coils: a + / - pair on the left stereo channel and one on the right. "When you use only four coils, you can use both mono signals and stereo signals, but not he 'alternating' signals intended for the 8 Coil Shakti. You can also use the two-phase sessions" (W/4_coil_placement.htm).
- Placement in the labelled photos: the L pair on the left of the head, the R pair on the right. Temporal and frontal: one coil above the other (left L- above L+, right R+ above R-). Temporo-frontal: side by side (front -, back +). Parietal: along the strip, one coil near the midline and one lower.
- Drive level: "The output level to drive the unit 'peaking' at the normal level of 10mG is .5 volts (4 and 8 coil units only)" (W/measurement.htm).

### 8 Coil Shakti

- Eight coils on a Velcro cap or bands, four per side of the head: on each side one + / - pair of the left channel and one of the right channel, set as a cross (one pair vertical, one horizontal). "You do NOT put all the left channel coils over the left side of you head" (W/8_coil_placement.htm). The chart puts the L pair top and bottom and the R pair front and back, with opposite signs on the two sides of the head; the page adds that it "does not matter" which channel is vertical, and the photos do not all carry the same signs.
- The signals are stereo and move between the two channels, so between the two pairs on each side, not between the hemispheres. Slow: "one 'switch' after one instance of the signal". Fast: "they switch channels every half-second"; "In lab tests, it emerged as the most effective" (W/rot_principles.htm). "There are also sessions where the signals don't alternate" (product page).
- Placements with their own pages: temporal, frontal, parietal, temporo-frontal, each "symmetrically". One-sided use: "you should use two of the coils to test each signal over each side, one side at a time, before you use the alternating signals over only one side" (W/8_coil_amygdala_consent.htm and the other 8-coil signal pages).
- Independent description: Gendle & McGrath 2012 p.4 [220] (hardware), p.11 [227] (0.1-1.0 µT in the middle of the headpiece).

### Shakti helmet (first version, discontinued)

- A helmet with three coils per side, driven from a stereo CD; left side left channel, right side right channel; "Stereo signals (different in each channel - but not alternating signals) should be used with this arrangement" (W/shakti_helmet.htm). The vendor's two descriptions of the coil positions differ (+ above the ear with - coils over the "frontal and temporal lobes"; or + temporal, - frontal, a third coil parietal). Tsang 2004 p.5 [1017]: "three sets of solenoids at the level of the prefrontal, parietal, and temporal lobes".
- Signals: burst-firing (amygdala) left, LTP (hippocampal) right, both at once; about 1 µT and 0.5 µT near the scalp (Tsang 2004 p.5-6).

### The named signals: numbers or name only

The slow hippocampal file name, read the same way (inference). amy_4000_3: 4000 ms between signals, 3-ms points (stated in words). ltp_1_1: "One millisecond between signals, and one millisecond for each point" (stated). ltp_24_16 would then be 24 ms between signals and 16-ms points: the same file more than five times slower, nearly without pause, which agrees with "same shape, but different proportions". The vendor does not say this in words.

### Session rules, in the vendor's words

- Length: "There are only four durations that are tested": 10 min (tests), 20 min, 30 min (series), 60 min (single sessions, at most once in three weeks). "Longer sessions are not advised" (W/safe_durations.htm).
- How often: "NEVER use Shakti two days in a row"; every 72 hours or once a week, three or six sessions, then three weeks off (W/two_ways.htm, threedays.htm, break.htm). Not back and forth between two session designs (W/how_often_is_safe.htm).
- Which side: amygdala signal left, hippocampal signal right; caudate has "no 'correct' side" (left arousal, right relaxation) (W/reversed.htm). One-sided sessions need a 10-min test per side first, unpleasant side first (W/testsession.htm). Sessions with the same signal on both sides need no test (W/getting_started_basic.htm, point 3).
- 60-min sessions: both temporal lobes, same signal both sides, blindfold, earplugs, silence ("substituting white noise won't work"); candidates amygdaloid (best tested), hippocampal, modulated 40 Hz ("not well-tested in practice") (W/two_ways.htm, hour.htm).
- Coils are not moved during a session, "except where indicated in an 'advanced procedure' or a two-phase session" (W/safe_placement.htm).
- Series over the temporal lobes and over the frontal lobes should alternate; parietal series should not be mixed with other placements (W/rot_temporal.htm, bi_parietal.htm).

## Places on the head that a source states

No source of this family gives a 10-20 or 10-10 site, an MNI or Talairach coordinate, or a measured distance for a coil. What is given:

- Two coils, both temporal lobes: "about an inch above each ear" (W/obe45_consent.htm; W/8_coil_mod40_consent.htm).
- Parietal strip: "about an inch behind the topmost point on the head, and then in a strip down to the top of the ears" (W/bi_parietal.htm); its upper part "from an inch behind the very top of the head to two inches from that point" (W/septum_consent.htm).
- Cerebellum: "Use the bony ridge at the back of your head"; "The Occipital Lobes are above the bony ridge on the back of your head. The Cerebellar vermis is just below it" (W/cerebellum.htm and its image).
- Frontal lobes: "one over each eye" (W/amy_hipp_sequence_consent.htm).
- Out-of-body placement: "one coil in front of and below the right ear, and one coil over the angular gyrus on the right side" (W/obe45_consent.htm).
- 8-coil cross over the temporal lobe: photos only (r_l_pos_neg.jpg, 8_coil_l_temporal.jpg on bands; temporal_hat.jpg on a cap, where the four coils almost touch). The size of the cross differs between the photos.
- Lab equipment: Koren boxes "over the temporal lobes (just above the ears)" (Tsang 2004 p.5 [1017]); solenoids "along the temporal plane" (Persinger 1994 p.2 [192]; Richards 1992 p.2 [668]; Gillis 1993 p.2 [169]); blocks "just above and in front of the ears" (Granqvist 2005 p.3).
- EEG recording sites, not coil sites: F7, T3, P3, O1, F8, T4, P4, O2 (Tsang 2004 p.3 [1015]).
- Gendle 2012: coils "over the temporal lobes as per manufacturer guidelines" (p.4 [220]); meter in the centre of the headpiece about 9 cm from the coils (p.11 [227]).

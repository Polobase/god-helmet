# Library notes: Shakti sessions

These are reading notes. They were taken in November 2026 while every program of the library was checked against its sources ([PRESET_AUDIT.md](PRESET_AUDIT.md)), and they are published as they were taken: one row per statement, with the page and, where it is short, the passage. Pages are PDF page numbers with the printed page in brackets, for example `p.3 [661]`. The last column says how a statement was read: `image` on the page image, `text` in the text layer only (scans confuse µT with mT and drop minus signs, so no decision rests on such a value alone), `web` on a web page. Statements about the vendor's products come from the vendor's own pages and are claims. Nothing here was measured on a helmet.

`B/` stands for `https://www.god-helmet.com/wp/winshakti/demo/shakti_demo/`, `R/` for `https://www.god-helmet.com/wp/winshakti/rotating/`. For web pages the "page" column gives the heading.

## Places on the head that a source states

- Tsang 2004 p.3 [1015] (text): EEG electrodes, not coils: F7, T3, P3, O1 and F8, T4, P4, O2. Coils: "three sets of solenoids at the level of the prefrontal, parietal, and temporal lobes" (p.5 [1017], image); Koren boxes "over the temporal lobes (just above the ears)" (p.5 [1017], image). No 10-20 names for coils.
- Gendle 2012 p.4 [220] (image): eight solenoids "over the temporal lobes as per manufacturer guidelines"; field checked at the centre of the headpiece, about 9 cm from the solenoids (p.11 [227]). No site names.
- Persinger 1994 p.2 [192] (image): "Within each side of the helmet, four solenoids were embedded along the temporal plane".
- Richards 1992 p.2 [668] (image): "four pairs of solenoids on each side along the plane of the temporal lobes".
- Persinger 2002 (Swann) p.4 [930] (text): eight solenoids "along the plane supported by a line between the midforehead and above the ears"; EEG at F7, F8, T3, T4, O1, O2.
- Tiller 2002 p.1 (text): "applied primarily over the right parietotemporal region".
- Blanke 2002 p.1 (text): subdural electrodes on the right angular gyrus; no scalp coordinate.
